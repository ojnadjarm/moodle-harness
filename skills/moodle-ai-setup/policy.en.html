Using the AI tools on this site

The AI tools send the text you choose to an AI provider selected by this organisation. Do not include personal data about yourself or others, grades, health or other sensitive information.

AI output can be wrong. Check it before you use or publish it; you remain responsible for what you submit.

Each person has an hourly limit of AI requests. Your requests are recorded on this site and can be reviewed by administrators.
